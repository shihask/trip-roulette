Access, permits & safety

Seasonal closure

Eravikulam closes each year for the tahr calving season. Check the official source for this year's dates.

  • PermitsNone needed for these stops. Park entry by ticket.
  • RoadsGhat roads with hairpin bends; heavy rain can bring advisories.
  • WeatherCooler than the coast. Carry a light jacket.
